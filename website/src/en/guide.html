SETUP & INTEGRATION GUIDE · v1.2.4-r1

From device inspection to installation and rollback

Only one Unicom wolverine unit was fully verified.

⚠️ Emergency calls are not supported or certified; keep another phone. On LTE-only networks 110/112/911 may be unreachable. Back up modem EFS independently and keep it off-device before writing; use at your own risk. Inspection logs are not restorable backups and fixed rollback cannot guarantee original values. Published v1.2.4-r1 artifacts lack the new edition gate and claims corrections and still permit one-click writes on oslo, wichita and keian; do not use them to write on unverified editions. The v1.2.4 protections have offline/mock evidence only.

01. Prerequisites & Verified Device Profile

  1. One SQW100-1 wolverine verified; oslo, wichita, keian, wseries and windermere are not hardware verified and the source defaults to read-only; rollback remains available.
  2. Source deployment also checks for legacy IMS daemons, MPSS.DI.4.0.1 and AAAAANAZM baseband identifiers, and firmware build_id AAB982; rollback bypasses deployment gates. Eligible post-reboot radio loss triggers an attempted fixed rollback, without guaranteeing original values or signal recovery. These protections were checked offline or with mocks only.
  3. Provisioned SIM: Insert an active 4G/5G USIM with VoLTE enabled on supported FDD-LTE bands and confirm LTE data attaches normally.
  4. Idle Phone State: Ensure no call is active, battery is above 30%, and use a reliable USB data cable.
Independently back up EFS off-device; the inspection log is not a restorable full backup.

02. Enable LineageOS 18.1 USB & Rooted Debugging

When using Method 1 (Windows EXE) or Method 3 (Portable CLI), Magisk is not required:

  1. Open Settings → About phone and tap Build number 7 times to unlock Developer Options.
  2. Open Settings → System → Advanced → Developer options.
  3. Turn ON Android debugging and Rooted debugging on the same screen.
  4. Connect via USB and accept the “Allow USB debugging?” RSA fingerprint dialog on the phone. (Once activation finishes, you can turn OFF “Rooted debugging” anytime—the stack ran natively on the tested device under SELinux Enforcing.)

03. Method 1: Windows Desktop One-Click EXE (Recommended)

  1. Download and launch qcom32-volte-1.2.4-r1-windows-x64.exe.
  2. Verify your device serial appears in “ADB Device” and click “1. Check Status (Read-Only)” (for non-Passport 32-bit Qualcomm devices, click “32-Bit Device Identification & AI Porting Check (Read-Only)” to run a 0-write 4-Gate audit and copy an AI porting prompt).
  3. On the published v1.2.4-r1 GUI, confirm the wolverine profile and select “2. Backup & Activate VoLTE (Recommended)”; despite the label, its inspection log is not a restorable EFS backup.
  4. The tool saves an EFS inspection log and hashes, then writes configuration, installs and reboots; the log cannot restore all original bytes.
  5. Click “Install Mobile Companion App” so you can monitor live IMS status directly on the phone screen while unplugged.

04. Method 2: On-Device Companion App

  1. Install Qcom32VolteManager-1.2.4-r1.apk (launcher icon: Qcom32 VoLTE).
  2. Without Magisk (Zero-su Live Dashboard + Read-Only AI Porting Check): Tap “Refresh Live Status” anytime to read carrier PLMN, rild_ims0, QMI_IMSA bearer/registration, and framework IMS state over content://org.octolab.qcom32ims.status, or tap “32-Bit Device Identification & AI Porting Check (Read-Only)” on any 32-bit phone to copy an AI porting report.
  3. On-device source allows activation only on wolverine; other Passport editions may inspect or roll back.

05. Method 3: macOS / Linux / Cross-Platform Portable ZIP

Extract qcom32-volte-1.2.4-r1-portable.zip (requires Python 3.9+ and adb):

python3 cli/volte_ctl.py self-test
python3 cli/volte_ctl.py list-models
python3 cli/volte_ctl.py status
python3 cli/volte_ctl.py inspect-device
python3 cli/volte_ctl.py deploy
python3 cli/volte_ctl.py install-manager
# Or launch the cross-platform Tkinter GUI:
python3 cli/gui.py

06. Method 4: LineageOS 18.1 ROM Integration & 32-Bit Porting

Free non-commercial community ROM builders can bake Qcom32VolteIms into /system_ext/priv-app/ using rom-integration/Android.bp and device-qcom32-volte.mk (retaining LICENSE, COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md, and Required Notice:):

mkdir -p packages/services/Qcom32VolteIms
cp -r src/android-ims-service/src src/android-ims-service/res src/android-ims-service/AndroidManifest.xml \
      src/qmi-imsa-probe rom-integration/Android.bp rom-integration/device-qcom32-volte.mk \
      packages/services/Qcom32VolteIms/
# Add to your 32-bit Qualcomm device.mk:
$(call inherit-product, packages/services/Qcom32VolteIms/device-qcom32-volte.mk)

Because Android ROM zips do not overwrite Qualcomm’s dedicated modem EFS partition, modem EFS provisioning must still be run once per handset. Paid/commercial ROM builds, paid flashing services, and reselling pre-flashed secondhand phones require a prior Commercial License (octoooo@octoooo.com). For onboarding OnePlus One, Xperia Z2/Z3, Galaxy S5, Mi 4, LG G3, and HTC One M8 via devices/<id>/profile.json, see MSM8974_PORTING_GUIDE.md and devices/README.md.

07. SIM swapping and verification limits

One 46001 unit verified for calls; Telecom 46003/46011 LTE and IMS registration only, calls untested, EPS-only fix 149 seconds without drops. Mobile untested; FDD B3/B8 theoretical, no TDD B38–41. SMS over IMS is not implemented; emergency calls, VoWiFi, video, supplementary services and non-Chinese carriers are unsupported or untested.

08. Live Call Acceptance Checklist

  1. Unplug the USB cable and verify Qcom32 VoLTE shows “VoLTE Stack Active & Registered”;
  2. Place an outgoing call in the stock Dialer: confirm clear two-way audio and verify the status bar stays on LTE / 4G;
  3. Receive an incoming call: confirm ringing, accurate Caller ID display (not “Unknown”), and clean hangup;
  4. Reboot the phone normally, wait ~25 seconds after unlock, and place another call to confirm hands-free boot recovery.

09. Fixed EFS cleanup

The published v1.2.4-r1 GUI labels this “Restore Factory Modem EFS & Uninstall”; it performs fixed cleanup, not per-device original-byte restoration. Or run:

python3 cli/volte_ctl.py rollback

Fixed cleanup writes 00 and deletes items; it does not restore original bytes from the inspection log.

10. Verify SHA-256 Checksums

# Windows PowerShell:
Get-FileHash "$HOME\Downloads\qcom32-volte-1.2.4-r1-windows-x64.exe" -Algorithm SHA256

# macOS / Linux:
shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/qcom32-volte-1.2.4-r1-portable.zip
